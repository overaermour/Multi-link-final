About the Club

Drama is one of the most transformative extracurricular activities a student can pursue. It builds confidence, empathy, and communication skills that are valuable in every area of life. The A.R.M.S. Drama Club rehearses in the school auditorium every Monday, working on scripts, improvisation exercises, voice projection, and stagecraft. The club performs at school cultural events, prize-giving ceremonies, and community shows throughout the year.

How to Join

The Drama Club is open to all A.R.M.S. students — no acting experience needed, just enthusiasm and a willingness to step out of your comfort zone. Come to the Auditorium any Monday at 3:30 PM. The Arts Department advisor will welcome you and introduce you to the current projects and exercises the club is working on.
