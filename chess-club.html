About the Club

Chess is far more than a board game — it is a training ground for the mind. Studies have consistently shown that regular chess play improves academic performance, logical reasoning, and emotional resilience. At A.R.M.S., the Chess Club meets every Friday in the library for practice sessions, friendly tournaments, and study of famous chess openings and strategies.

How to Join

The Chess Club is open to all A.R.M.S. students regardless of experience. Visit the library any Friday at 3:45 PM to join the session. Beginners will receive guidance on the basic rules, piece movements, and opening principles before progressing to full games and competitive play.
