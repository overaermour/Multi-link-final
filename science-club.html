About the Club

The Science Club at A.R.M.S. gives students the opportunity to conduct experiments that go beyond what is covered in regular CSEC classes. Members investigate scientific questions, design and carry out experiments, analyse results, and present their findings. Past projects have included chemistry demonstrations, biology dissections, physics experiments, and environmental studies of the Essequibo Coast.

How to Join

The Science Club welcomes all A.R.M.S. students with a curiosity for how the world works. Visit the Science Laboratory any Tuesday at 3:30 PM to join a session. Members are encouraged to bring ideas for experiments and topics they would like to explore. No lab experience is necessary — just an inquiring mind.
