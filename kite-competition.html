Kite Flying Competition

Calling all creative minds. Showcase your craftsmanship at the Annual A.R.M.S. Kite Making and Flying Competition.

Back to Events

Calling all creative minds! Join our Annual Kite Making Competition and showcase your craftsmanship, creativity, and skill. Build your kite from scratch and take to the skies on the A.R.M.S. grounds.

Event Details

Date: April 17, 2026

Time: 4:00 PM

Location: Anna Regina Secondary School Grounds

Attire: Casual

Category: Events

What You Will Learn

Beyond the fun and competition, the Kite Flying Competition develops real skills. Participants develop memory and concentration as they plan and build their kites, and express their creativity through design, colour, and construction. It is a celebration of ingenuity and Guyanese culture.

Competition Rules

  • All kites must be handcrafted by the participant. NO PRE-MADE KITES!
  • Each kite must adhere to a $10,000 to $15,000 budget.
  • Every kite must successfully fly during the competition to qualify for judging.

About the Anna Regina Media Team

We are the Anna Regina Secondary School Media Team, a dynamic group of exceptional students responsible for capturing and sharing the vibrant events and activities across our campus. Through photography, videography, and writing, we are dedicated to serving our school community by highlighting its achievements, creativity, and spirit.
