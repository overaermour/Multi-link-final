Expansion Era

The decades of growth that transformed A.R.M.S. into one of Region 2's most complete educational institutions.

Back to History

As the Essequibo Coast grew, so did A.R.M.S. New buildings, stronger academic programmes, technical streams, and a thriving sports and clubs culture defined an era of rapid expansion that cemented the school's place at the heart of Region 2 education.

New Buildings and Facilities

New classroom blocks were constructed to accommodate students from across the Essequibo Coast and surrounding villages. Sports facilities including a gymnasium and hard courts expanded the school's physical education programme, giving students space to compete and develop athletically at inter-school and regional levels.

Academic Programme Growth

A.R.M.S. expanded its academic offerings significantly, adding advanced CSEC subject options and strengthening both the academic and technical/vocational streams. Science laboratories were upgraded to support chemistry, biology, and physics at a higher standard. The school library grew into a well-stocked resource centre used by students from across the community.

Technical and Vocational Streams

A distinguishing feature of A.R.M.S. has always been its commitment to technical and vocational education. During this expansion period, the school invested heavily in workshop facilities and technical programmes that gave students NCTVET/NVQJ qualifications alongside or in place of CSEC. These streams ensured that every student left A.R.M.S. with meaningful, employable skills.

Sports and Clubs Come Alive

Clubs and sports programmes flourished during this era. Cricket, Football, Volleyball, and Athletics became pillars of school life, with A.R.M.S. teams competing in the Essequibo Schools Sports Association competitions. Chess, Drama, Science, and Debate clubs were established, giving students a rich extracurricular life that complemented their academic work. The annual Sports Day became a flagship event on the Essequibo Coast calendar.
