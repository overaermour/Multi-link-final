Arrival Day Celebration

Anna Regina Multilateral School honours the heritage and contribution of Indo-Guyanese people on Indian Arrival Day.

Back to Events

Each year on Indian Arrival Day, Anna Regina Multilateral School comes together to honour the courage, sacrifice, and enduring cultural contribution of Indo-Guyanese people, a community whose story is woven into the fabric of the Essequibo Coast.

Event Details

Date: May 5, 2026 (Indian Arrival Day)

Time: 10:00 AM

Location: A.R.M.S. Main Hall

Attire: Cultural Attire encouraged

Category: Cultural Events

The Significance of Arrival Day

Indian Arrival Day, observed on May 5, commemorates the arrival of the first indentured labourers from India to British Guiana on May 5, 1838. These men, women, and children left their homeland under difficult circumstances and built new lives on Guyanese soil, contributing immeasurably to the cultural, agricultural, and social life of the nation. Their descendants make up a vital part of Guyana's diverse population and the Anna Regina community.

How A.R.M.S. Celebrates

The Arrival Day Celebration at A.R.M.S. features cultural performances, traditional music and dance, displays of Indo-Guyanese heritage, and reflections on the journey of the indentured diaspora. Students are encouraged to dress in traditional cultural attire and participate in the programme. The event is a powerful reminder of Guyana's multicultural identity and the values of respect, unity, and shared history that A.R.M.S. upholds.
