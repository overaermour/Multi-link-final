Modern Achievements

Celebrating the accomplishments, awards, and milestones that define A.R.M.S. today.

Back to History

In 2021-2022, the Caribbean Examinations Council declared Anna Regina Multilateral School the CXC CSEC School of the Year, the highest recognition in Guyanese secondary education. That honour reflects a school that has never stopped pushing forward.

CXC CSEC School of the Year 2021-2022

The most significant recent milestone in A.R.M.S. history came when the Caribbean Examinations Council (CXC) declared Anna Regina Multilateral School the CSEC School of the Year for the 2021-2022 examination cycle. This prestigious national recognition, awarded based on outstanding student performance across CSEC subjects, placed A.R.M.S. at the top of Guyanese secondary education and was celebrated across the Essequibo Coast community.

Consistent CSEC Excellence

A.R.M.S. has been consistently ranked among the top-performing schools in Region 2 and nationally, regularly placing students in Guyana's top CSEC results. The school's graduates go on to the University of Guyana, regional universities including the University of the West Indies, and professional careers across Guyana and the Caribbean. High pass rates in Mathematics, Sciences, English, and technical subjects reflect the school's comprehensive academic programme.

Sports Achievements

On the sports field, A.R.M.S. competes at inter-school and regional levels across Cricket, Football, Volleyball, Athletics, and Badminton. The school has produced athletes who have gone on to represent Region 2 in national competitions, and Sports Day remains one of the most anticipated events of the school year, drawing the broader Anna Regina community together.

Mashramani and Cultural Excellence

A.R.M.S. is also known for its vibrant cultural programme. Students participate in Mashramani celebrations tied to Guyana's Republic Day (February 23), cultural shows, and performing arts events that showcase the creativity and talent of the student body. The Drama Club has produced performances that have earned recognition at inter-school cultural competitions.
