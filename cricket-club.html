About the Club

A.R.M.S. has a long and celebrated history in regional cricket, with the school's teams competing at the Essequibo Schools Sports Association level and beyond. The Cricket Club trains every Thursday on the Cricket Ground, covering batting drills, bowling techniques, and fielding practice under the supervision of the Sports Department.

How to Join

The Cricket Club is open to all A.R.M.S. students. Come to the Cricket Ground on any Thursday at 3:30 PM in PE clothes. Whether you want to bat, bowl, or keep wicket, the club has a place for you. Students who demonstrate consistent skill and commitment are considered for inter-school tournament squads.
