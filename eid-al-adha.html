About Eid al-Adha

Eid al-Adha, known as the Festival of Sacrifice, is one of the two major Islamic holidays and is observed by Muslims worldwide. It commemorates the willingness of Ibrahim (Abraham) to sacrifice his son as an act of obedience to God's command. For Muslim students and families at A.R.M.S. and across the Essequibo Coast, it is a time of prayer, reflection, gratitude, and community gathering.

A United School Community

At A.R.M.S., cultural and religious celebrations are a mark of pride. The school draws students from across the Essequibo Coast and reflects the full diversity of Guyanese society, Afro-Guyanese, Indo-Guyanese, Amerindian, and Mixed heritage students learning and growing together. Eid al-Adha is an opportunity for the whole school to come together, learn about each other's traditions, and reaffirm the values of respect, tolerance, and unity that define the A.R.M.S. community.
